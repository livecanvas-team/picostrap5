# Picostrap

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A WordPress starter theme with Bootstrap, NinjaBootstrap and browser-based Sass compilation. Its options panel helps site builders configure the theme, understand its frontend cleanup and work with Sass.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The panel lives inside WordPress admin. Preserve native navigation, permissions, existing theme mods and Customizer synchronization. Extras require explicit saving. Performance numbers must state their measurement scope; no fabricated speed scores or site-wide savings.

## Brand Commitments

The requested options redesign is dark, modern, slightly cyberpunk, imaginative but concrete and clearly organized. Use the Picostrap logo and picostrap.com as visual references. Keep this treatment scoped to the options panel.

## Evidence on Hand

Theme source, local CSS/JS files, installed WordPress asset files, and the existing experimental CSS reducer. The official logo is saved at inc/options-page/assets/picostrap-logo.svg. No site-wide performance benchmark is supplied.
